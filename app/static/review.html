    Smart category suggestions

    The app learns only from classifications you choose. It suggests categories for similar transactions, but never approves or syncs them by itself.

    Start fresh

    Remove all local app records: statements, transactions, categories, cashbook connection, sync history and activity. Your original Excel workbook is not changed.

    Help

    Simple guidance for your daily cashbook workflow.

    1. Connect your cashbook

    Choose the existing WCED legacy .xls cashbook. The application checks its layout before it is used.

    2. Import a statement

    Import your Standard Bank statement. The statement must reconcile before transactions can be processed.

    3. Review transactions

    Choose the right income or expense category. Similar transactions can be selected together only when you explicitly approve it.

    4. Sync and open Excel

    Sync reviewed transactions safely, then open the cashbook in Excel. A backup is created before workbook changes.

    Need support?

    If something goes wrong, note the error message and download diagnostics from Settings. Your accounting records are not removed.
